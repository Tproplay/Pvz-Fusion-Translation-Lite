<align=center><size=130%>Plants VS. Zombies: Fusion - Update 4.1 Changelog</size></align>

1. Odyssey: Evolved Rework
- Earn Points by completing rounds to purchase plants, upgrade the shop, and reroll
- Synthesize duplicate cards or upgrade the shop to acquire higher-tier cards
- Equip seed packets with various equipment for powerful bonus stats
- Fusion plants inherit the tiers and gear of both component materials
- Zombies reaching the house deduct HP instead of causing an instant defeat

4. Level Updates
- Super Random: Question Mark Showdown: Paint portraits of random plants recruited by Artist Sunflower and use them to fight off incoming zombies

5. New Modifiers
- Shrink Ray — Mini Professor Z!

6. Balance Adjustments
- Blover Pot, Levitater, Sunny Pumpkin: Numerical stat buffs
- Threepeater Cannoneer series: Attack speed nerfed
- Buck-nut, Steel Pumpkin, Football-nut: Significantly increased HP recovery when consuming matching metal objects
- Magnet Blover: Converts digested metal into item drops
- Calamity-shroom & Avarice-shroom: Reworked effects. Removed Avarice-shroom's restriction in Odyssey Cursed Difficulty
- Trident Zombie: Added a slowing effect when inflicting Wet status
- Pozeidon: Can bite and amplify Wet debuff like Trident Zombie; giant tridents create whirlpools that attract projectiles
- Trident Jugger-nut Gargantuar: Throwing tridents no longer requires HP to fall below 50%

7. Other Improvements
- Random Mode no longer immediately triggers the next wave when total zombie HP on the lawn is low
- Polished expression animations for select plants
- The Magic Brush recognition plugin is now server-deployed, eliminating device-dependent discrepancies
- Improved the pacing of points and modifier acquisition in Classic Odyssey Mode

8. Bug Fixes & Quality of Life
- Fixed a bug where Floating Super Gatling Pea vanished upon scene transition
- Fixed a bug where Bountiful Points erroneously received Cursed Difficulty point multipliers
